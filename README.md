One page. Every region. Every field. A single 1024×1024 front page that
tells the whole of **Friday 27 November 2026** — built for the make.fun
bounty [news-image-20261127](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261127).

## What's on the page

- **42 stories, 7 regions** — North America 6 · Latin America 3 ·
  Europe 8 · Middle East 6 · Africa 5 · Asia 10 · Oceania 4. Every story
  happened, or was first reported, on Fri 27 Nov 2026; anything older is
  named in the crimson **DATE CHECK** strip instead of being passed off
  as today's news.
- **The lead:** the EU summit ends deadlocked over the bloc's $1.76tn
  long-term budget and the €49bn loan for Ukraine — Kyiv's winter
  funding left unresolved heading into December.
- **The big numbers band:** $1.76tn EU budget deadlocked in Brussels ·
  100+ drones in Russia's overnight wave on Kyiv and Kharkiv · $350bn
  five-year defence plan Japan is weighing · 17 civilians killed in
  Blue Nile drone strikes · 9 injured in the Bangkok refinery blast ·
  1 seat New Zealand's ruling coalition fell short by.
- **Certainty, labelled:** every lead carries a chip — CONFIRMED as
  reported, REPORTED single-source, CLAIM for one side's account — with
  the legend printed in the footer, and a source on every story.
- **The day on the map:** a dot-matrix world map with numbered pins for
  the located stories, plus an hour-by-hour UTC timeline of the day.
- **DATE CHECK, in the open:** three big stories that broke on
  Thursday 26 Nov — Ebola topping 12,000 cases in Congo, the Eagles'
  39–38 win over the Cowboys, and the FIFA cyberattack — are flagged in
  the crimson strip at the foot of the page rather than dressed up as
  Friday news.

## How it was made

Stories were harvested from the wires (Reuters · AP · BBC · Al Jazeera ·
Radio Dabanga · PANA) and date-checked one by one. The page is typeset
in HTML/CSS and rendered with WeasyPrint; the ten illustrations are
original flat-editorial artwork in a locked palette of ink black, warm
cream, crimson and slate. Headlines were shortened at selection time so
nothing on the page is ever clipped.
